`stays` records the top edge of each of its selectors' first match, then samples them until `until` appears and for 600 ms after. It fails if any top moves by more than a pixel, if a target isn't on the page, or if `until` never appears within 15 s. With `releasing` it lets go of the `stall` step for that glob only after its first measurement, so the change it watches can't land before it has a starting point. `receipt-finish-phone` stalls the finished job that way, then keeps a phone's job card and the model below it in place while the print finishes.
