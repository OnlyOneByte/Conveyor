`keyScrolls` needs its target to have focus already (use `tabTo` first). It fails unless focus shows a ring at least 2 px thick (two adjacent pixels on each edge line) that changes by 3:1 within 6 px of at least 90% of the target's straight edges, compared with the same target blurred, and unless PageDown raises its `scrollTop`. Each browser call it makes is bounded at 5 s, so a page that stops answering fails at once, naming the step. It gives focus back so the frame shows the ring, as `profile-details-scrolls` does for an overflowing Details block.
