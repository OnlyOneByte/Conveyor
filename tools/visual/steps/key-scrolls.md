`keyScrolls` needs its target to have focus already (use `tabTo` first). It fails unless focus shows a ring that changes by 3:1 within 6 px of at least 90% of the target's straight edges, compared with the same target blurred, and unless PageDown raises its `scrollTop`. It gives focus back so the frame shows the ring, as `profile-details-scrolls` does for an overflowing Details block.
