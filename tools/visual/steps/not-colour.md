`notColour` fails when its target's text colour is the colour that the CSS custom property `token` resolves to where the target sits, or close to it. Both colours are drawn to a 1×1 canvas, and the step fails when no sRGB channel differs by more than 16/255, the threshold `compare.ts` counts as a visible change. So a tint or a `color-mix` a few percent off the token fails too, and the check holds in both themes. `jobs-failed-note` uses it to keep a failed job's reason out of `--err`.
