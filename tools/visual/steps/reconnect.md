`reconnect` with `"editor"` lifts the block an earlier `offline` step with `only: "editor"` put on the editor's chunks, so their next download succeeds. With `delayMs`, each chunk is held that long before it loads. `profile-editor-reconnect` uses both to prove Try again loads exactly one editor however many times it is pressed while the download runs. With a route such as `"/printers"`, it lifts what an earlier `lazy` step put on that route's on-demand chunks, so their next download goes straight through, as `printers-dialog-down` does to prove the next click after a failed load opens the form.
